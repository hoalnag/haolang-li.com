    故事Story

    Story故事
    Logline

    On the day he brings his son along, an unlicensed cab driver in New York takes hits from a broken air conditioner, a difficult passenger and the TLC, while trying to stay the man his son believes he is.

    故事梗概Synopsis

    Wang Qiang drives an unlicensed minivan out of Flushing, Queens. One freezing morning, with a drunk passenger asleep in the back and the heater dead, his wife calls. There’s no school today for Eid, and she has to go to work. So their nine-year-old son rides with him.

    A rat has chewed through the van’s wiring, and the repair costs $150. To make it back, Wang takes a pickup at JFK, where a TLC officer writes him two tickets. The lawyer puts the damage at four thousand dollars. His son sees all of it. At sunset, when the light gets in his father’s eyes, the boy hands him the pilot sunglasses he bought with the gas money.

    “也就是混个日子。”“Just getting by.”

    王强，48岁，福建福清人。六年前，他借了十五万美元，沿着南美线路来到了美国。背负着债务的他在法拉盛地带贴过膜，做过后厨炒锅，当过服务员。他的英文很差，能做的也只有这些不用说英文的活。他最大的梦想就是把妻儿接到纽约，开一家自己的店铺，有自己的车，并在长岛买一栋自己的别墅。

    他现在已经记不清最开始那几年在外州工作，每周往返的生活是怎么熬过来的。幸运的是，他成功拿到了身份，也把妻儿接了过来，并在法拉盛租了一间足够一家三口居住的小家。

    王强人至中年，身体大不如前。后厨的日子实在不好熬，他的身体无法再承受油烟和一周七天的固定排班。于是，六个月前，经一位同乡介绍，他加入了一个黑车车队，并租赁了一辆七座车。干这一行的好处在于时间自由，还没有平台抽水，赚到的都是自己的。这样算下来，他每月的流水能到一万五千美金，扣除房租、油费、拮据的生活费、孩子的学费等，一个月能剩下一千美金。

    王强知道自己现在的生活已经来之不易，所以那些可能会将他拉入深渊的黄赌毒他是不会沾一点的。他见过了太多这样的例子。生活的粗糙早已让他忘掉最开始在长岛买别墅的梦想。开店的念头也一直没敢动，不是没想过，是不敢，怕全盘皆输，他身上还担着太多责任。“也就是混个日子”是他经常挂在嘴边的一句话。他现在最大的希望就是自己的小孩能上个好大学，也许长大去参军，因为听说退伍军人在美国会被优待。

    Wang Qiang is 48, from Fuqing in Fujian Province. Six years ago he borrowed $150,000 and came to America by way of South America. With that debt on his back, he worked his way through Flushing: putting screen protectors on phones, cooking on the line in restaurant kitchens, waiting tables. His English is poor, so those were the only jobs open to him. His dream was to bring his wife and son to New York, open a shop of his own, own a car, and one day buy a house on Long Island.

    He can no longer remember how he got through those first years working out of state, going back and forth every week. In the end he got his papers, brought his wife and son over, and rented a small apartment in Flushing just big enough for the three of them.

    Now in middle age, his body isn’t what it used to be. He couldn’t take the kitchen anymore, the grease and the smoke and the seven-day weeks. So six months ago, through a man from his hometown, he joined a fleet of unlicensed cabs and started renting a seven-seat minivan. He sets his own hours, and with no app taking a cut, whatever he makes is his. In a month he brings in about $15,000. After rent, gas, a tight household budget and his son’s school costs, about $1,000 is left.

    Wang knows how hard it was to get here, so he stays away from the women, the gambling and the drugs that could pull him under. He has watched it happen to too many people. Life has worn him down enough that the house on Long Island has long since slipped his mind. He has never dared to open the shop either. It isn’t that he hasn’t thought about it. He is afraid of losing everything, and too many people depend on him. “Just getting by,” he likes to say. What he hopes for most now is that his son gets into a good college, or maybe joins the military, because he has heard veterans are treated well in America.

    在他的印象里，爸爸是最厉害的人。In his mind, his father is the most impressive person there is.

    小孩，男，9岁，正在上小学，中文和英文都会说一些。

    他出生的时候，爸爸并不在身边。打记事起，他就听妈妈说，爸爸去了大洋彼岸。街坊邻居都很看得起爸爸：树挪死，人挪活，王强到了美国没几年，就把欠大家的钱连本带利还清了。他们还说，要是换作别人找他们借十万在福清做生意，他们是不会借的，那样的人没出息。所以在小孩的印象里，爸爸就是最厉害的人。

    他已经不记得自己是怎么和妈妈一起来到美国的，只知道是坐大飞机来的。现在他每天都能在房间里看到飞机呼啸而过。可是爸爸妈妈从来不让他跟学校里的同学讲这件事。

    爸爸妈妈好像都很忙。有时候放学，是别的同学的妈妈先把他接走；晚些时候，爸爸或妈妈总会有一个人匆匆赶来接他。

    在他眼里，爸爸每天能赚很多钱。坐在车上时，他总爱打开座位中间的储物格，翻看爸爸这一天赚的钱。

    The Kid is nine and in elementary school. He speaks some Chinese and some English.

    His father wasn’t there when he was born. For as long as he can remember, his mother told him his father had gone across the ocean. The neighbors back home thought the world of Wang. A tree that moves dies, a man who moves lives, they said, and sure enough, within a few years of getting to America, Wang paid back everything he owed them, with interest. If anyone else had come asking for a hundred thousand to start a business in Fuqing, they said, they would have turned him down. A man like that would never amount to anything. So in the Kid’s mind, his father is the most impressive person there is.

    He doesn’t remember how he and his mother ended up in America. He only knows they came on a big plane. Now he can see planes roar past from his room every day. But his parents never let him talk about it with the other kids at school.

    His parents always seem busy. Some days another kid’s mom picks him up after school, and later on one of his parents comes rushing over to get him.

    As far as he can tell, his father makes a lot of money every day. Whenever he rides in the van, he likes to open the compartment between the seats and go through the money his father has made that day.

    运镜Camera movement

    导演笔记Director's notes
    • 晃与静的对比：手持的晃动和固定机位的静止相互对照，反映当下处境的情绪强度。
    • 中景以内、人物在动：用手持。摄影机是画面里的第三个角色，像一只眼睛一样参与。
    • 中景以外：以三脚架或稳定器为主，需要时摇镜头（横摇、俯仰），被动地跟随画面里的运动。
    • 需要时也可以用手持拍远景。
    • 留意主观镜头，以及它带来的视角切换。
    • 不用滑轨、轨道车和斯坦尼康。
    • Shaky against still: the contrast between handheld movement and stillness reflects the emotional intensity of each situation.
    • Closer than a medium shot, with characters moving: handheld. The camera is the third character in the shot and performs as an eye.
    • Wider than a medium shot: mainly tripod or gimbal, with pans and tilts as needed to follow the movement passively.
    • Handheld wide shots when needed.
    • Keep an eye on POV shots and the shift in perspective they create.
    • No sliders, dollies or Steadicam.

    景别、镜头与构图Shot sizes, lenses and composition

    导演笔记Director's notes
    • 以中长焦为主，50mm 以上。长焦压缩空间，把人和环境挤进同一个平面，给场面调度留出更多余地。
    • 光圈不开大过 T5.6，保持大景深：人和城市一样清楚。
    • 寻找框中框：车窗、后视镜、挡风玻璃、门框。
    • 寻找剪影的机会。
    • 首要目标不是把人物的脸曝光好。
    • 车玻璃：反光、污渍和雾气都是画面的一部分。
    • 200–800mm 超长焦段落，参考《一战再战》（待定）。
    • Mostly mid-to-long focal lengths, above 50mm. Long lenses compress space, press people and their surroundings into one plane, and leave more room for mise-en-scène.
    • Never open up wider than T5.6. Deep focus: the people and the city equally sharp.
    • Look for frames within the frame: windows, mirrors, the windshield, doorways.
    • Look for chances to silhouette.
    • Exposing the characters' faces well is not the main objective.
    • Car glass: reflections, grime and fog are part of the image.
    • 200–800mm extreme telephoto passages, after One Battle After Another (TBD).

    色彩Color

    导演笔记Director's notes
    • 写实，不过度饱和，甚至可以是萧瑟的。
    • 尊重法拉盛原本的颜色。
    • 接受颗粒感的画面，可以考虑胶片模拟。
    • 小孩出场以后，颜色才慢慢多起来。
    • Realism. Not oversaturated, even bleak.
    • Respect the original colors of Flushing.
    • Open to grainy images and film emulation.
    • Color appears more once the Kid enters.

    光线Lighting

    导演笔记Director's notes
    • 不用柔光源做主光。
    • 太阳是主光源，需要时再补适当的柔光。
    • 寻找从缝隙里透进来的阳光。
    • 太阳是片中的第三个角色，代表温暖和希望。
    • 写实是布光的核心原则。
    • 车灯是另一个主光。
    • No soft sources as key.
    • The sun is the key light, with proper soft fill only as needed.
    • Look for sunlight coming through cracks.
    • The sun is the third character, standing for warmth and hope.
    • Realism is the central principle.
    • Car lights are another key.

    情绪与质感Mood and texture

    导演笔记Director's notes

    柔和的苦甜。A soft bittersweetness.

    • 前半段属于主角的困倦。
    • 气氛萧瑟：阳光很明显，但冬天依然寒冷。
    • 太阳最后变成一种让人难以直视的东西。
    • The first half belongs to the protagonist's drowsiness.
    • A bleak atmosphere: the sunlight is pronounced, but it is still winter and still cold.
    • By the end, the sun becomes something too hard to look at.

    行车
    演员表演时不开车，车内戏用拖车或在封闭的低速路段拍。机器和灯用专业车载支架固定。

    警察制服

    拍摄许可

    凌晨开工
    保证两次开工之间的休息，收工后的疲劳驾驶要有人负责。
    Driving
    Actors never drive while performing: in-car scenes go on a process trailer or a closed, low-speed road. Cameras and lights on proper vehicle mounts.
    Child actor
    The Kid is nine. He needs a New York Child Performer Permit, with a Notice of Use filed two business days ahead. Ten minutes’ rest every four hours, and an adult on set throughout. NYS Department of Labor
    The Kid's shots

    Police uniforms on city streets need the NYPD Movie & TV Unit on location; clear TLC uniforms with the Film Office. MOME
    Permits
    Street filming goes through MOME’s student permit process. MOME student permits
    Props and effects
    Herbal prop cigarettes, never lit with the Kid inside; the chewed insulation and the vomit are props.
